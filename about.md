# About Quzzi

## Catalyst ✈

The author's initial drive behind this work was to prove that Quantum Annealing could be used to solve certain hard problems that airlines of various sizes are confronted with on a daily basis. Despite using powerful classical computing solvers used by the larger airlines, these systems continue to struggle to find optimum results within operational deadlines, especially in a world where strategies are in constant increasing flux.

## Classical computing challenges

Having personally written and deployed classical solvers for some of these problems, the author's experience with the constant battle to work around the impracticality of brute-force solvers and "right-sizing" heuristic algorithms to obtain viable operational results for NP-hard problems made him wish for a "chip" that would handle the combinatorics and solution-landscape exploration.

## Experimental code

Discovering the D-Wave Quantum Annealing technology in 2018, it appeared as a wish come true, leading to learning about and experimenting with Quantum computing in general — and more specifically how to use the D-Wave Quantum Computer to solve the Airline Crew Trip use case.

## What to expect from the Quzzi site

Quzzi aims at making Quantum Annealing solvers more accessible and as simple as possible to anyone, by sharing experience, code, examples, and training on methodologies.

Quzzi is listening to anyone interested in this work — and specifically what they wish to learn from it.
