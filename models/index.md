# Models

Prototype solvers demonstrating Quantum Annealing on the D-Wave Quantum Hybrid Solver, from a real-world aviation problem down to small teaching examples. Start with a toy problem if you're new to QA, then move to the airline model.

You can load any of this code directly into a D-Wave Leap account: [Load models in Leap IDE](https://ide.dwavesys.io/#https://github.com/q-zee/DWave).

| Model | Difficulty | Formulation | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Airline Crew Trip](/models/airline-crew-trip/) | Real-world | BQM (CQM in progress) | Deployed in production prototype (VYouPointAero) |
| [Wolf, Goat & Cabbage](/models/wolf-goat-cabbage/) | Toy / teaching | CQM | Complete |
| [8 Queens](/models/8-queens/) | Toy / teaching | BQM and CQM (comparison) | Complete |
